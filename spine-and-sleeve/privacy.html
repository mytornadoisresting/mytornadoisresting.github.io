Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

In short: Spine & Sleeve doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and I never see your library.

Your library stays on your iPhone

The items you add, your cover photos, ratings, notes and loans are stored on your iPhone. They're included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's or your computer's privacy terms cover. If you export a backup file, you choose where it's saved.

Looking things up

To identify an item, the app sends what you scan or search for (a barcode, title, artist, author or catalogue number) directly from your iPhone to these public catalogue services:

  • MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive (MetaBrainz Foundation)
  • Open Library (Internet Archive)
  • Wikidata, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons (Wikimedia Foundation)
  • RAWG
  • UPCitemdb

To show covers and details for items already in your library, the app also sends those items' catalogue IDs to the same services. Nothing else about you or your library is sent.

Like any website, these services receive your device's IP address with each request. The app identifies itself to them by name, with the contact address on this page, as some of them require. Their own privacy policies cover what they receive. Nothing is sent to me.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read barcodes and to take photos of covers. Read text from a cover photo reads the words on a photo using Apple's text recognition on your iPhone; the photo isn't sent anywhere. Photos you choose from your library are picked with Apple's photo picker, so the app only sees the photos you choose, and they stay on your iPhone.

Reminders

Loan reminders are notifications scheduled on your iPhone. The app asks for permission to send notifications only when you first set a reminder.

Discogs import

If you import a file exported from Discogs, it's read on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to Discogs.

API keys

If you add your own key for a service in Settings › Lookup services, it's stored in your iPhone's Keychain and sent only to that service.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stores on your iPhone. Backup files you've exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

This website

This website uses no cookies or analytics. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
