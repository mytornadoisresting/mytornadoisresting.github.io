Spine & Sleeve

Your records, CDs, films, games and books, catalogued on your iPhone.

What it does

  • Scan or search to add CDs, vinyl, DVDs, Blu-rays, 4K discs, games and books, and pick the exact edition you own.
  • Do I own it? Scan a barcode in a shop to check your collection before you buy.
  • Wishlist, loans and reminders: keep track of what you want and who's borrowed what.
  • Box sets are added as one box, with each film as its own title.
  • Ratings, watched and listened flags, genres and filters to find things quickly.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your library is kept on your iPhone.

Questions

How do I back up my library, or move it to a new iPhone?

Go to Settings › Export backup and save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive in the Files app. It includes your titles, copies, ratings, loans, box sets and your own cover photos.

On the new iPhone, install Spine & Sleeve and choose Settings › Import backup. Importing adds what's missing and never deletes anything, so it's safe to import the same file twice.

Your library is also included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups.

A barcode isn't found. What can I do?

Some barcodes aren't in any public catalogue, which is common for UK films and older games. Choose Search and type the title instead. The barcode you scanned is saved with whatever you pick, so it's recognised next time.

If nothing matches at all, choose Not listed? Enter details manually.

Why don't most films have a cover?

Film details come from Wikidata, which is free for anyone to use, but most film posters are copyrighted, so only a few free ones are available (from Wikimedia Commons). For the rest, tap the cover and take a photo of your own copy, or use Search the web (below).

How do I use a cover I've found on the web?

Tap the cover and choose Search the web. Safari opens an image search for the item, such as "Heat 1995 Blu-ray cover". Touch and hold the picture you want and choose Copy, then come back to Spine & Sleeve, tap the cover and choose Paste cover. You can crop it before it's saved, and it's kept as your own cover.

This works for records, films, games, books and box sets.

How do I add a box set?

Go to Add › Search › Films & TV › Add a box set, or scan the box's barcode. For a series, use Find the film series and untick any films that aren't in your box. For a mixed box, add each film by searching. A disc in a different format from the rest of the box can be changed per film.

The box's page lends, sells or returns the whole box at once.

How do loan reminders work?

On an item's page, choose Lend to someone…, enter a name and, if you like, a due date with Remind me on the due date. The reminder is a notification scheduled on your iPhone. Choose Mark as returned when it comes back.

Why are some games missing box art?

Game details and box art come from RAWG. The app shares a free monthly allowance between everyone who uses it, and when that runs out, games are found on Wikidata without box art until the next month. You can add your own free RAWG key in Settings › Lookup services to avoid the shared limit.

Items I added earlier are missing details. Can I fill them in?

Settings › Update all items from catalogues looks everything up again and fills in only what's missing. Anything you've typed yourself is never overwritten.

Can I import my Discogs collection?

Yes. Export your collection or wantlist as a CSV file on discogs.com, then choose Settings › Import from Discogs. The file is read on your iPhone, and records already in your library aren't added twice.
